PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kropp ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Portia Apps & Games, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Kropp mobile application (the "Service").

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT & DEVICE PERMISSIONS

1.1 Media & User-Generated Content (Processed Locally)

Kropp is a video editor and voice-over recording application. All media files, including:

  • Videos recorded or imported from your device gallery
  • Audio and voice-over recordings captured using the microphone
  • Video thumbnails, waveforms, and edited projects

are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not upload, host, or transfer your personal video or audio creations to our servers.

1.2 Device Permissions

To provide core editing and recording functionality, the app may request access to:

  • Camera: To record video clips directly within the application.
  • Microphone: To record voice-overs and audio for your video projects.
  • Photos / Storage: To import media from your gallery and save rendered/exported video files to your device.

1.3 Analytics and Diagnostic Data

We use third-party analytics and diagnostic tools to understand app usage and improve stability. These services may collect technical metadata such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and system language
  • Anonymous user identifiers and app interaction events
  • Crash logs, stack traces, and error reports

1.4 Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription or unlock premium features, in-app transactions are securely handled by the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and our purchase management provider (RevenueCat). We do not collect or store your payment details or credit card information.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain video and audio editing features
  • Manage project autosaves, undos, and export workflows locally
  • Process subscriptions and manage access to Pro/Premium features
  • Monitor, analyze, and troubleshoot app performance and crash reports
  • Serve contextual or personalized advertisements (where applicable)

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The app integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

Your media projects, cache files, and settings remain stored on your local device until you delete them or uninstall the app. We implement commercially reasonable security standards to protect all diagnostic and configuration data.
