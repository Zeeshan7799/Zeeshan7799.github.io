Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Rocket Run, a rocket survival game developed by Canis Studio, handles information. The Android package is com.canis_studio.rocket.

At a glance: core gameplay works offline. Personal bests, settings, and a small number of diagnostic run reports stay on your device. Google Play Games, when enabled, handles online identity and leaderboards. The current game has no advertising, in-app purchases, or separate developer analytics SDK.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores personal best altitude and scores, control preferences, steering sensitivity, and haptic settings. When online leaderboards are enabled, it may also store your Google Play Games player identifier and the highest unsent altitude so a failed submission can be retried for the correct player.

For development and troubleshooting, the game keeps up to six recent local run reports. These may contain the route seed, game and engine version information, device model, platform, screen size, control mode, run timing, altitude, fuel, collisions, and sampled rocket movement and input. Reports are replaced as new runs are recorded. The game does not automatically upload these reports to us.

2. Google Play Games and online leaderboards

Some builds enable Google Play Games on Android. When enabled, Google authenticates your Play Games profile and receives submitted Arcade altitude scores. Your gamer identity, profile visibility, and leaderboard presentation are controlled by Google and your Play Games settings. We do not receive your Google password.

Google's Play Games SDK may process gamer identity, service analytics, and diagnostic data. Google states that its service data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. See Google's Privacy Policy and Play Games Services data disclosures. Builds with the feature disabled do not submit leaderboard scores through the game's integration.

Core gameplay does not require a separate Rocket Run account. The game does not use friends-list access, cloud saved games, or a developer-operated gameplay backend.

3. Permissions, advertising, and purchases

Touch input and optional motion sensors support steering. The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location for gameplay. Network access supports Google Play Games and opening this policy. Haptic feedback uses device vibration where supported.

The current version does not display ads, sell personal information, or offer in-app purchases. It does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or advertising SDKs. Google Play and the operating system may independently process installation, security, or crash information under their own settings and policies.

4. How information is used and shared

Local information supports gameplay, preferences, personal bests, and troubleshooting. Online score and player information supports Google Play Games leaderboards when available. We do not automatically send local gameplay reports to third parties.

If you contact support, your email address, message, and any files you choose to send are used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary personal information. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages; hosting-related technical information is handled according to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Retention and deletion

Personal bests and settings remain locally until reset, cleared, or removed. The in-game Reset Progress control clears local personal bests; it does not delete local diagnostic files, pending leaderboard submissions, or Google's leaderboard records. To remove the game's remaining local data, clear its app storage or uninstall it. Operating-system backups may retain or restore data according to your device settings.

Manage or delete Google Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google Account settings, including Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request; you may contact us to request deletion of information you supplied to us.

6. Teenagers and children

Rocket Run is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address the request.

7. Security and changes

Local information is stored in the app's device storage, and online services use their own security safeguards. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date, and provide additional notice when appropriate.
