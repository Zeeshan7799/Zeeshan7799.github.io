PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoBreak-X-Days (also shown as Unbroken or UnBroken X Days, "the app") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information, the legal bases we rely on, and the rights you have, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR YOUR DATA

The app is developed and published by Canis Studio, an independent developer, which acts as the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at any time at zeeshan.7799@gmail.com.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information you create in the app

  • Habit data, including habit names, schedules, colors, icons, start and end dates, streak data, archive and pause status, and related settings.
  • Completion data, including completion dates and optional notes.
  • Reward and progress information related to milestones and unlock states.
  • Reminder times and app preferences (for example paper, pen and ink styles).

2.2 Account information

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we receive the following through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo
  • Your account identifier (user ID)

2.3 Subscription and purchase information

If you buy NoBreak-X-Days Pro, payment is handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. To manage your subscription we use RevenueCat, which receives your app user ID (your account identifier), the products you bought, prices, currency, purchase and renewal dates, and subscription status.

2.4 Usage analytics

With your permission where the law requires it (see section 4), we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. This includes:

  • In-app events, such as creating, completing, archiving or deleting a habit, opening screens, changing appearance settings, signing in or out, and starting, completing or cancelling a purchase (including product, price and currency). Habits are referred to by random identifiers, never by name or notes.
  • A Firebase app instance identifier and, on Android, the device advertising identifier where available.
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.

2.5 Diagnostic and crash information

To keep the app reliable we use Firebase Crashlytics, which collects:

  • Crash logs, stack traces and error reports
  • Device model, operating system and app version
  • A Crashlytics installation identifier and basic device state at the time of the crash (for example free memory and orientation)

2.6 Advertising information

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Pro subscribers do not see ads. To serve and measure ads, Google and, if you consent, its advertising partners may process your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device information, and your interactions with ads.

2.7 Notification information

If you enable reminders, the app stores your reminder times and notification permission status on your device.

2.8 Drawings

If you mark completions with a manual drawing, the image is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded or synced.

4. CONSENT, ADVERTISING AND ANALYTICS CHOICES

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (certified under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) before personalised ads are used. In that case:

  • Usage analytics (section 2.4) stays switched off until you consent to storing and accessing information on your device. If you decline, analytics is not collected.
  • Personalised ads are used only if you consent. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads selected using limited data.
  • You can change or withdraw your choices at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Settings. Withdrawing consent stops further processing based on consent but does not affect processing that already took place.

Outside these regions, analytics is enabled by default. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. SERVICE PROVIDERS WE USE

We share personal information only with the providers below, who process it to deliver their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, backend and database hosting on Google Cloud, Analytics, Crashlytics) and Google Sign-In – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob and, with your consent, its certified advertising partners – policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) – revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play (payments, app updates and in-app review prompts) – policies.google.com/privacy

6. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the providers in section 5, we share information only:

  • When you choose to export or share your exported file
  • If required by law, regulation or legal process, or to protect rights, safety and security

7. WHERE DATA IS STORED AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Most of your data stays on your device. If you sign in, synced data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States. Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. Where personal data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to countries without an adequacy decision, these providers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

  • Data on your device remains until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Account and synced data is kept while your account exists and is permanently deleted when you delete your account.
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically by Google.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to manage your subscription and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

If GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Rectify inaccurate data (most data can be edited directly in the app)
  • Erase your data (see section 10)
  • Restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability: use the in-app export to get your habits and completions as a JSON file
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including non-personalised advertising
  • Withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Settings
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the EEA, see the list of EU authorities; in the UK, the ICO)

To exercise any right, email us at zeeshan.7799@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request. Requests about data held only by Google (for example ad data) can also be made directly to Google.

10. ACCOUNT DELETION

You can delete your account and all associated server-side data at any time:

  • In the app, in Settings > Delete Account (shown while you are signed in), or
  • On the web at unbroken-flutter.web.app/delete-account

Deletion removes your account and synced habit, completion, settings and reward data from our servers. Data stored only on your device remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Subscription records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for legal and accounting purposes may be retained as described in section 8. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

11. NOTIFICATIONS

Reminders are local notifications generated on your device. The app does not send remote marketing push notifications. To schedule reminders reliably, the app may ask for notification permission and, on some Android devices, exact alarm and restart-related permissions.

12. EXPORTS

If you use the export feature, the app creates a JSON file on your device containing your habit and completion information, including notes. You control what happens to that file. If you share or upload it yourself, that sharing is your responsibility.

13. SECURITY

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to synced data requires your signed-in account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The app is not directed to children under 13. In the EEA and UK, people below the age of digital consent in their country (between 13 and 16) should use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it.

15. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will show a new Effective Date. If a change materially affects how we use your personal data, we will let you know before it takes effect (for example in the app or on its store listing) and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
